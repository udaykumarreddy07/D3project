Executive Abstract for Institutional Leadership

Autonomous engineering colleges operating under the University Grants Commission (UGC) Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) require comprehensive analytics to track student learning competencies, identify students requiring remedial intervention, and generate authentic academic transcripts. Legacy university portals suffer from static tabular reports, intrusive popups, and lack of privacy separation.

This project engineers an institutional-grade web platform powered by D3.js (v7) and Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) featuring:

  • 100-Student Multi-Branch Cohort: Realistic modeling of 100 students across 5 engineering disciplines (20 CSE, 20 ECE, 20 EEE, 20 MECH, 20 CIVIL) with official roll numbers (22A91A...).
  • Direct In-Graph Touch Intelligence: Touching any donut slice or bar immediately displays the underlying breakdown and constituent student roster directly inside the graph area with zero modal popups.
  • 5-Engine Visual Analytics Studio: Alluvial conduit flows, KDE ridgeline density waves, 5-axis parallel coordinates, institutional orbit constellations, and concentric radar HUDs.
  • Strict Role-Based Privacy: Faculty access 360° cohort oversight, while Students are restricted strictly to an exclusive personal observatory (Spider Radar Web, Bullet Visualizer, Biometric Attendance Dial, Semester SGPA Trajectory).
  • Autonomous Progress Card: 1-click A4 printable verified CBCS semester marksheet.

SECTION 2 & 3: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & 5-ENGINE STUDIO

2.1 Modular Engineering Architecture

The portal is engineered as a clean, zero-dependency client-side architecture using standard Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), D3.js v7, and Vanilla CSS3 Glassmorphism:

Figure 3.1: Live Faculty 5-Engine Visual Analytics Studio at http://localhost:3000 showing Alluvial Conduit Flows, Batch Sparkline KPIs, and Branch Filters.

3.1 The 5 Specialized Visual Analytics Engines

  1. 🌊 Alluvial Conduit Flows: Models all 100 students in organic beeswarm distributions using D3 force collision (d3.forceCollide(6.5)). Splines bundle via cubic Bézier curves (M x0,y0 L xMid,y0 C ...) into UGC qualification sinks: Distinction (>= 85%), First Class (70-84%), Pass Class (35-69%), and Arrears Alert (< 35%).
  2. 🏔️ Kernel Density Ridgeline Waves: Overlapping probability density estimators modeling mark distributions across Maths, Science, English, and Programming.
  3. ⚡ 5-Axis Parallel Coordinates: Traces multi-dimensional trajectory lines across 5 vertical axes with branch color-coding, exposing departmental strengths and weaknesses.
  4. 🌌 Institutional Orbit Tree: Radial planetary graph connecting central autonomous college hubs to 5 departmental spokes (CSE, ECE, EEE, MECH, CIVIL) with multi-ring student glyphs.
  5. 🍩 Concentric Radar HUD: Futuristic telemetry circular visualizer with live crosshairs and statutory 75% attendance threshold rings.

Section 4: Direct In-Graph Touch Intelligence BIET CSE Department

SECTION 4: DIRECT IN-GRAPH TOUCH INTELLIGENCE

A signature innovation of this project is the Direct In-Graph Touch Intelligence Engine (.in-graph-tray), fulfilling the user's requirement to inspect underlying data inside the graph itself without separate popups:

Figure 4.1: Direct In-Graph Touch Drawer sliding over the Donut Chart, displaying all 29 Grade O students, their roll numbers, marks, and 1-click marksheet inspection with zero external modal overlays.

4.1 Operational Mechanics of In-Graph Inspection

  • Interactive Center Donut Core (#donutCenterVal): Touching or hovering over any donut slice dynamically lights up the center circle with the count, percentage, and grade badge in neon cyan. Touching the center resets the view.
  • Sliding In-Graph Drawer (#trayGradeChart): Touching any grade slice (e.g. Grade O) slides an obsidian glassmorphic tray directly inside the #cardGradeChart container.
  • 4 High-Precision Telemetry Metrics: Displays Constituent Count (29), Mean Score (92.7%), Top Score (99%), and Attendance Avg (93%).
  • Scrollable Constituent Roster: Lists every matching student with their official roll number, department, score, and instant marksheet inspection button.
  • One-Click Dismissal: A top-right [✕ Back to Donut] button allows immediate dismissal back to full chart view.
  • Multi-Chart Extension: Identical in-graph sliding trays are integrated into the Subject Average Chart (#traySubjectChart), Gender Demographics (#trayGenderChart), and Biometric Attendance Chart (#trayAttendanceChart).

SECTION 5: ROLE SEPARATION & STUDENT OBSERVATORY

To enforce strict privacy and prevent peer score exposure, the portal separates access privileges into two isolated views:

Figure 5.1: Student Personal Academic Observatory (Sneha Devi, Rank #1, SGPA 10.00) featuring Radar Spider Web, Bullet Visualizer, Biometric Attendance Dial, and SGPA Trajectory.

5.1 Student Personal Visual Suite (4 D3.js Visualizers)

  1. 🕸️ D3 Subject Competency Spider Web (#studentRadarChart): 4-axis cyber radar web contrasting the student's cyan polygon against the amber dashed Class Average benchmark.
  2. 📊 D3 Personal Benchmark Bullet Visualizer (#studentBulletChart): Horizontal multi-layer bullet bars comparing the student's score against class average pin and subject topper diamond.
  3. ⏱️ D3 Concentric Attendance & Exam Clearance Dial (#studentAttendanceDial): Concentric glowing circular arc gauge with the statutory 75% examination clearance threshold line.
  4. 📈 D3 Multi-Semester SGPA Trajectory Curve (#studentTrajectoryChart): 6-semester progression line (Sem 1 to Sem 6) showing longitudinal academic improvement.

5.2 Verified CBCS Marksheet Progress Card

Students can view and print an autonomous college progress card formatted to official A4 standards with autonomous letterhead, course table, credit points, digital security hash, and 1-click PDF print.
