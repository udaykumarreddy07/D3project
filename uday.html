👑 UGC CBCS Grade & Evaluation Indicators
Grade O (Outstanding / Topper Tier)
Golden Amber: Represents aggregate scores ≥ 90% (Grade Point: 10.0). Indicates top-tier institutional excellence and First Class with Exemplary Distinction.
Range: 90.0% – 100% | Grade Point: 10.0
Grade A (Excellent) & Passed Status
Emerald Green: Represents aggregate scores 80%–89% (Grade Point: 9.0) and confirmed subject passing clearance (Score ≥ 35 in all subjects).
Range: 80.0% – 89.9% | Grade Point: 9.0
Grade B (Very Good / First Class)
Electric Cyan: Represents aggregate scores 70%–79% (Grade Point: 8.0). Demonstrates strong concept mastery and above-average academic rigor.
Range: 70.0% – 79.9% | Grade Point: 8.0
Grade C (Good / Second Class)
Neon Violet: Represents aggregate scores 60%–69% (Grade Point: 7.0). Indicates standard competence fulfilling all autonomous learning outcomes.
Range: 60.0% – 69.9% | Grade Point: 7.0
Grade D (Pass / Borderline Clearance)
Coral Orange: Represents scores 35%–59% (Grade Point: 6.0). Cleared minimum passing score but positioned near the boundary threshold.
Range: 35.0% – 59.9% | Grade Point: 6.0
Grade F (Arrear / Remedial Required)
Crimson Red: Signals an academic alert: student scored < 35 in one or more subjects (Grade Point: 0.0). Requires supplementary re-examination.
Cutoff: < 35 Marks | Grade Point: 0.0 (Fail)
